# TokiToki

TokiToki is a gacha-based RPG game where players roll for Tokis—unique characters
with customizable stats and abilities—through a gacha system. Each Toki can be
equipped with various skills and items, which are essential for battles against monsters
or other players. Players can strategically select and enhance their Tokis, forming teams
to engage in turn-based combat where they choose which Toki will perform specific
actions, such as attacking, defending, or healing. The game focuses on strategy,
collection, and customization, allowing players to create powerful teams and progress
through challenges by optimizing their Tokis' abilities and synergies
